# Content attribution

The demo content is a small, checked cut of the Mottendr word pack: the 300 most frequent words of its first
release as the word list, and a dictionary of the 500 most frequent words. It is licensed
[CC BY-SA 4.0](./LICENSE.md). It is an adaptation: structure, senses, short definitions and examples were
rewritten and reorganised for learning.

## Sources and credit

- **Word list.** Bowen (the author's own word list), rewritten and extended by Bowen through a scripted
  rewriting process (every batch checked by scripts and an independent review; not a professional linguistic
  review).
- **Wiktionary contributors**, through Kaikki / Wiktextract (<https://kaikki.org/dictionary/>) and **WikDict / DBnary**
  (<https://www.wikdict.com/>): lexical data, senses, pronunciation, forms and translations. CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Démonette-2** (Namer, Hathout et al. 2023, *Lexique* 33): derivational families and complete simple-tense
  tables. CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Larousse** (<https://www.larousse.fr/>): used only to check facts (which auxiliary a verb takes, exceptions,
  disputed senses). Entries link to the Larousse page that was checked; compound-tense tables are built by rule
  (auxiliary plus past participle) and no Larousse text is copied. Larousse content is not under CC BY-SA and is
  not part of this licence.
- **Lexique 3.83** (New, Pallier, Brysbaert, Ferrand; <https://www.lexique.org/>): word frequency, used to order
  the words (the average of film-subtitle and book lemma frequencies). CC BY-SA 4.0.

The dictionary is made from the same checked entries (the 500 most frequent words), so its definitions are the
entries' definitions, with the same sources. It is not an extract of the source dictionaries and has no forms or
conjugation tables of its own. No endorsement by the source authors is implied.
