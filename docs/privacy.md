# Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Mottendr (the iPhone app and the web demo). Last updated: 15 November 2026 · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 15 日。

## English

**Mottendr does not collect any data about you.** There is no account, no server of ours that receives your data,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app does not use any third-party SDK.

- **Your study data stays on your device.** Your progress, notes and settings are stored only on your iPhone (in the
  app's own storage) or, for the web demo, in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see them.
- **Backups are yours.** If you export a backup, it is a file you create and share where you choose (for example to
  Files). The app never uploads it anywhere.
- **Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app asks permission only when you turn the
  reminder on. No push-notification service is used.
- **Speech** uses the voices installed on your device.
- **No network use by the app.** The word list and dictionary are inside the app. The app opens a web page only if
  you tap a source link on an entry page; that page belongs to its own website and its own policy.
- **The web demo** is a static website. Its own code sets no cookies and has no analytics or third-party scripts.
  The network provider that delivers it (Cloudflare) may keep ordinary technical request logs, such as the IP address
  and time of a request, and may measure page loads in aggregate (Cloudflare Web Analytics). We do not use any of this
  to identify you. The iPhone app has none of this: it makes no network requests.
- **Children.** The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

**Contact:** open an issue at <https://github.com/beandecode/mottendr/issues> (see [Support](support.md)).

## 中文

**Mottendr 不收集任何关于你的数据。** 没有账号，没有接收你数据的服务器，没有统计分析、广告或追踪，应用里也没有任何第三方 SDK。

- **学习数据只在你的设备上。** 你的进度、笔记和设置只保存在你的 iPhone（应用自己的存储空间）里；网页演示则保存在你设备上浏览器的本地存储里。我们看不到这些数据。
- **备份归你所有。** 你导出的备份是你自己生成的文件，由你决定存到哪里（例如“文件”）。应用不会把它上传到任何地方。
- **提醒**是在你设备上设定的本地通知。只有你打开提醒时，应用才会请求通知权限；不使用任何推送服务。
- **朗读**使用你设备上已安装的语音。
- **应用本身不联网。** 词表和词典都在应用内。只有当你点击词条页上的来源链接时，才会打开一个网页；该网页属于对应网站及其自己的政策。
- **网页演示**是一个静态网站，它自己的代码不设置 Cookie，也不含统计或第三方脚本。递送它的网络服务商（Cloudflare）可能保留常规的技术请求日志（例如请求的 IP 地址和时间），也可能汇总统计页面加载情况（Cloudflare Web Analytics）。我们不会用这些来识别你。iPhone 应用没有这些：它不发起任何网络请求。
- **儿童。** 应用不向任何人（包括儿童）收集任何信息。

如果本政策有变化，新版本会连同新的日期在此发布。

**联系方式：** 请在 <https://github.com/beandecode/mottendr/issues> 提交问题（见[支持](support.md)）。
