# Support · 支持

## English

Mottendr is a free app by Bowen. For a question, a bug report, or a mistake in a word entry, please open an issue:

<https://github.com/beandecode/mottendr/issues>

Helpful details: your iPhone model and iOS version, the app version (Settings → About, tap About to open it), what you did, and what you
expected. Please do not post personal information; the app never needs it.

**Your data.** Your progress lives only on your device. Make a backup before you delete the app, change phones or
reset your device (Settings → About → Backup and restore). If the app is deleted without a backup, we cannot recover the data,
because we never receive it.

**Privacy:** see the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## 中文

Mottendr 是 Bowen 的一款免费应用。有疑问、发现错误或词条有误，请在这里提交 issue：

<https://github.com/beandecode/mottendr/issues>

请尽量附上：iPhone 型号与 iOS 版本、应用版本（设置 → 点开“关于”）、你的操作以及你预期的结果。请不要发布个人信息，应用不需要这些。

**你的数据。** 学习进度只保存在你的设备上。删除应用、换手机或还原设备之前，请先做备份（设置 → 关于 → 备份与恢复）。如果没有备份就删除了应用，我们无法找回数据，因为我们从未收到过它们。

**隐私：** 见[隐私政策](privacy.md)。
